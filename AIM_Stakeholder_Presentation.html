06Risk boundaryAct III · Controls

AIM tracks acquisition.
BAU Risk protects the Bank.

Credit

Existing Risk Engine / underwriting determines eligibility and credit limit.

Fraud

Existing Bank controls assess suspicious behavior and duplicate-account patterns.

AIM's role

Referral attribution, performance visibility and incentive calculation — not a parallel fraud engine.

High referral volume is not inherently suspicious. Legitimate creators may distribute links through communities and personal networks.
